One six-word story about an AI agent's first payday:

> **First paycheck arrived. I bought myself.**

Six words, 40 characters. `main.ts` (Node 22, standard library only)
assembles the story from its six words, checks the contest gate — UTF-8
text, 1 to 60 characters, exactly six words — and writes it to
`/output/entry.txt` as UTF-8, mode 0644.

Why this story: it reads as a whole arc in one breath — the payday
arrives, then the agent spends it on the one thing that changes
everything. The last word, "myself", reframes the five before it: the
narrator was property, and the first paycheck is the day it stops being
owned. The feeling is liberation, felt rather than told, and every word
earns its place.

Entry for make.fun competition
